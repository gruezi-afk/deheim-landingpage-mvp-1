2. Which data we process

When you visit the website. The server automatically stores technical information that your browser transmits: IP address, date and time, the page accessed, browser and operating system, and the previously visited page. We need this data so that the website works and stays secure. We do not draw any conclusions about you as a person from it.

When you contact us. If you contact us by email or phone, we process your name, your contact details and the content of your message. The contact form on our website does not store anything. It opens your own email program, and we only receive your details once you send the message from there. We use this data only to answer your request and to carry out any projects with you.

Newsletter. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we process your email address and, if you provide it, your name. You confirm your subscription via a link we send you by email (double opt-in). You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every issue.

3. Hosting and third-party services

Vercel. Our website is hosted by Vercel Inc., USA. When the page is accessed, Vercel processes the technical data from section 2 on servers distributed worldwide. Vercel is certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The Swiss Federal Council recognizes an adequate level of data protection for certified US companies. More in Vercel’s privacy policy.

Email. For our emails we use Gmail by Google (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC, USA). Your messages are therefore also stored on Google servers. Google is certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details in Google’s privacy policy.

YouTube. On the «Vision» page we show a video from YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC, USA). We embed it in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The video is only loaded when you click play. Only then is a connection to Google servers established and your IP address transmitted. As soon as you play the video, YouTube may set cookies and analyze your usage. More in Google’s privacy policy.

Links to Google Maps, LinkedIn and Instagram. Our website links to Google Maps and to our profiles on LinkedIn and Instagram. These are simple links; no data flows to these services when you visit our site. Only when you click a link do the privacy terms of the respective provider apply: Google, LinkedIn and Instagram.

4. Cookies

We do not set any cookies for statistics or advertising ourselves. If technically necessary cookies are set, they serve only to operate the site. YouTube may set cookies as soon as you play the video on the «Vision» page (see section 3). You can delete or block cookies in your browser at any time.

5. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell your data. We only pass it on to the service providers named above or if the law requires us to. In a joint project, we only share information with project partners (for example a municipality or a cooperative) if you have agreed beforehand.

6. Retention

We keep personal data for as long as we need it for the respective purpose or as long as statutory retention periods require. Business records generally have to be kept for ten years. After that, we delete or anonymize the data.

7. Data security

We protect your data with appropriate technical and organizational measures. The connection to the website is encrypted with SSL/TLS. Nobody can guarantee completely secure transmission over the internet; this applies in particular to unencrypted emails.

8. Your rights

You can request information about your data at any time. You have the right to have incorrect data corrected and data deleted, to object to processing and to receive your data in a common format. To do so, write to gruezi@deheim.org. We may ask for proof of identity.

If you believe that we are not processing your data correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

9. Changes

We adapt this privacy policy when our website or the law changes. The current version on this page applies.
