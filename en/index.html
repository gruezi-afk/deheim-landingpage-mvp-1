We turn strangers
into neighbors

Deheim transforms residential buildings into thriving communities. By helping residents co-create shared experiences and lasting neighborhood traditions, we foster belonging and make properties more desirable places to live.

For property managers, cooperatives, developers, and municipalities that want higher tenant retention, better occupancy, and happier residents.

Deheim fills the void between anonymous new developments and full-commitment housing cooperatives.

More than one in three people in Switzerland feel lonely at least sometimes. Cooperatives have year-long waiting lists. Shared spaces in new buildings stand empty. People want to belong, but they are wary of commitment. Deheim designs the formats, hosts the events, and takes the pressure off.

Source: Obsan / Federal Statistical Office, Swiss Health Survey

Our Services

Every event is designed, hosted, and documented by us. Visual proof of social connection in your neighborhood.

  1. 01

    Neighborhood Onboarding

    For new developments

    Before or shortly after move-in, we bring residents together in a workshop to shape their neighborhood’s culture and first traditions. Then everyone meets at a welcome party with local producers and good food.

    Let’s talk

    Scope: 2 workshops, 1 party

  2. 02

    Neighborhood Activation

    For managed properties that need a fresh start

    Many neighbors have lived next door for years without meeting. We create easy, low-threshold ways to meet: a workshop, a farm visit, a co-creative dinner. Then we help residents turn what works into lasting traditions. More on request.

    Let’s talk

    Scope: 3 workshops, 1 party

  3. 03

    Neighborhood Pizza Party

    For a quick and easy first step

    The easiest way to start. We bring the ovens, our crew, local ingredients, and music. A short workshop over the dough first, then the courtyard opens for everyone: grab a pizza, or make your own.

    Let’s talk

    Scope: 1 party
