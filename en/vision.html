Connection instead of consumption.

More than one in three people in Switzerland feel lonely at least sometimes. Yet most of us live door to door with people who could make our lives richer. We build housing for individuals and wonder why it never becomes a neighborhood.

We believe community is not a matter of luck. It can be designed. Where neighbors know each other, people feel safer and happier, newcomers settle in faster, and we share more and buy less. That is good for people, good for the property, and good for the planet.

Deheim builds the social infrastructure of housing: not the walls, but what happens between them. With simple formats that bring people to one table, and with traditions a neighborhood carries on by itself. Our goal: that one day “Deheim” stands for a way of living where you know your neighbors.

Dubai, 2021

How it all began

It started with a question put to three students at EHL Hospitality Business School: what does hospitality look like in 2050? Lukas Lauber, Eve Mignot, and Stefano Abedum de Lima Hanzawa answered with SEVCCO, “Creators of Communities”: hospitality not as a service for guests, but as a force that creates community. Housing that connects people instead of isolating them, that shares resources instead of consuming them, and that does not entertain residents but enables them to shape their own community. With it they won the Sustainable Hospitality Challenge at AHIC in Dubai in 2021: best overall concept from over 40 universities worldwide.

What Deheim does today was already in that concept. Stage one: operators organizing community for residents. Stage two: residents shaping their community themselves. Stage three: neighborhoods that carry themselves. “Come for cost, stay for community,” the pitch said. We dropped the first part and made the second our profession.

The idea became COOLINARIK first, where young people, migrants, and seniors have been cooking and serving together ever since. And COOLINARIK became Deheim: the same method, brought to where people live.

Deheim is dedicated to Eve Mignot, who started this idea with us.
